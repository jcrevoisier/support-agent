## Returns
Customers can return an item within 30 days of delivery, if it is unused and in its original packaging. Items that have been worn or used cannot be returned unless they are faulty. Return shipping is free: customers start a return from their account page.

## Refunds
Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 business days after the returned item arrives at our warehouse. Shipping costs are refunded only when the item was damaged or wrong.

## Shipping
Orders ship within 2 business days. Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 business days and express delivery takes 1 to 2 business days. An order that has not arrived 10 business days after shipping is treated as lost: we send a replacement or a full refund.

## Damaged or wrong items
Customers must report a damaged or wrong item within 14 days of delivery, with a photo. We send a replacement or a full refund, and the customer does not need to send the item back.

## Exchanges
Customers can exchange an unused item for a different size or color within 30 days of delivery. Exchanges are free.
